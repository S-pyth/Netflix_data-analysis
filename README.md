# Netflix_data-analysis

The analysis focuses on understanding how Netflix’s catalog has evolved across different dimensions such as content type (Movies vs TV Shows), ratings, genres, release year, country, and duration. Multiple visualization techniques—bar charts, line charts, box plots, and count plots—are used to uncover relationships and growth trends.

**Key insights include:**

Movies constitute a higher share of Netflix’s library compared to TV Shows.

The United States and India are the top content-producing countries.

Most content is rated TV-MA and TV-14, reflecting Netflix’s focus on mature audiences.

Content additions peaked around 2018–2019, highlighting Netflix’s major expansion phase.

The average movie duration ranges from 80–120 minutes, with higher-rated films tending to be longer.

**🔍 Key Features**

Visualization of Netflix’s content distribution by type, rating, and country.

Analysis of content growth trends over the years.

Examination of movie duration patterns by rating.

Insights into genre popularity and audience targeting.

Professionally formatted PDF report summarizing visual findings and business implications.

**🛠️ Tools & Technologies**

Programming Language: Python

Libraries: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Development Environment: Jupyter Notebook

Visualization Output: PNG charts and compiled PDF report

**💡 Business Impact**

The findings from this analysis can help understand Netflix’s strategic focus, content diversification, and market trends. By identifying dominant genres, countries, and audience ratings, the project provides insights that can guide data-driven decisions in content creation, marketing, and regional expansion strategies.
